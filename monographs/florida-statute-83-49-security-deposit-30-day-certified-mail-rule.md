## Executive Summary
Under Fla. Stat. § 83.49(3)(a), a Florida landlord has 15 days to return the full security deposit—or 30 days to send written notice by Certified Mail of intent to impose a claim. Missing the 30-day Certified Mail deadline forfeits the landlord's right to deduct a single dollar from the deposit.

## Technical Breakdown & Actionable Protocol
Even if a landlord claims physical damage, Florida appellate precedent confirms failure to mail the statutory notice by Certified Mail within 30 days extinguishes the right to withhold any portion of the security deposit.
